# Ingredients

- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- ½ cup (100 g) brown sugar
- **⅔ cup (160 mL) purified olive oil**
- 2 large Earth eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ½ cup chocolate chips *(HIGHLY RECOMMENDED FOR MAXIMUM HUMAN SUBJUGATION!)*

---

# Instruments of Production

- 8×8 or 9×9 baking pan
- Large mixing bowl
- Rubber spatula
- Oven preheated to **350°F (175°C)**

---

# Phase One: Construct the Chocolate Engine

> **ZIM:** Place the sugars into a bowl! Pour in the olive oil and whip them together until they resemble delicious industrial sludge!

Add:

- Eggs
- Vanilla

Beat until smooth and glossy.

> **ZIM:** Continue mixing until the disgusting Earth proteins surrender completely!

---

# Phase Two: Powder of DOOM

In another bowl combine:

- Cocoa powder
- Flour
- Salt
- Baking powder

Slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients.

Mix only until the flour disappears.

## ⚠️ WARNING

**DO NOT OVERMIX!**

**DO NOT TEST ZIM'S PATIENCE!**

Fold in the chocolate chips.

---

# Phase Three: Thermal Domination

Spread the batter evenly into your greased pan.

Bake:

| Pan Size | Time |
|----------|------|
| 8×8 | 32–36 minutes |
| 9×9 | 28–32 minutes |

The brownies are finished when a toothpick comes out with **moist crumbs attached.**

If the toothpick comes out perfectly clean...

> **YOU HAVE GONE TOO FAR, FOOLISH EARTH CREATURE!**

---

# Phase Four: The Most Difficult Mission

Remove the brownies from the oven.

Allow them to cool for **30–45 minutes.**

> **ZIM:** YES... YOU MUST WAIT.

Warm brownies are unstable.

Cooling allows the chocolate matrix to solidify into its final weaponized form.

---

# ZIM'S Approved Upgrades

- 🧂 Sprinkle flaky sea salt on top before baking.
- ❄️ Refrigerate overnight for maximum fudginess.
- 🔥 Microwave each brownie for **10 seconds** before eating to reactivate maximum deliciousness.
